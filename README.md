## Methodology & Descriptive Statistics
The analysis evaluates key metrics including Open, Close, High, Low prices, and Trading Volume.

### PepsiCo Summary
* **Mean Closing Price:** $157.39
* **Standard Deviation:** 9.75 (Consistent, controlled movements)
* **Trading Volume:** ~6.38M shares

### Coca-Cola Summary
* **Mean Closing Price:** $64.93

## Key Conclusions
* **PepsiCo:** Offers stability, lower risk, and consistent growth, making it ideal for long-term wealth accumulation.
* **Coca-Cola:** Exhibits higher volatility and frequent price swings, offering short-term speculative opportunities and higher growth potential.
